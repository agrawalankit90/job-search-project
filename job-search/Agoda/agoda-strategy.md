## Step 2 — Guiding Policy

### Positioning Angle
Lead with: **"Consumer loyalty mechanics are universal — the playbook I ran for Walmart+ maps directly to travel loyalty, repeat booking, and LTV optimization."**

Don't apologize for not being in travel. Reframe: Walmart+ is a harder loyalty problem (commodity grocery vs. aspirational travel) and the playbook transferred across two international markets.

### Application Channel Priority
1. **Referral** — highest conversion; find Agoda employees (especially Indian PMs in Bangkok) on LinkedIn
2. **Direct apply** — Agoda careers site; use tailored resume
3. **Recruiter outreach** — identify Agoda TA team on LinkedIn; send Variant A/B outreach message
4. **LinkedIn Easy Apply** — lowest priority; only if the above are done

### Non-negotiables
- Role must be Senior PM or above (not Associate/PM-1)
- Bangkok + relocation support confirmed before accepting offer stage
- Compensation: negotiate in USD or request relocation package; don't anchor on INR

## Step 5 — Interview Loop Expectations

Agoda typically runs 4–6 rounds:
1. **Recruiter screen** (30 min) — fit, timeline, comp, relocation
2. **Hiring manager** (45–60 min) — product sense, past work, leadership
3. **Product case study** (take-home or live) — likely growth/experimentation scenario
4. **Cross-functional panel** (2–3 back-to-backs) — data, design, engineering perspectives
5. **Bar raiser / leadership** (45 min) — strategic thinking, culture fit
6. **Offer + negotiation**

Prepare for: a live A/B test design question, a funnel optimization case, and a behavioral on driving cross-functional alignment without authority.

## Risk Flags

- **Long loop + competition**: Apply early; Agoda pipelines can take 6–8 weeks — don't wait for Agoda before activating other Tier 1/2 targets in parallel
- **Relocation logistics**: Start visa research early (Non-B + work permit process for Indian nationals in Thailand)
- **Comp adjustment**: Bangkok offers may be 20–30% lower in INR terms; evaluate total comp including housing allowance and quality-of-life factors
