# Agoda — Company Research Brief
**Role Target:** Senior Product Manager | Bangkok
**Prepared:** June 2026

---

## Company Overview

- **Parent:** Booking Holdings (same group as Booking.com, Priceline, Kayak, Rentalcars)
- **HQ:** Bangkok, Thailand — one of the largest tech employers in SEA
- **Scale:** 2M+ properties listed, 90M+ app downloads, operations in 200+ countries
- **Business model:** OTA (Online Travel Agency) — hotel-first, expanding into flights, experiences, and packages
- **Engineering org:** ~4,000+ engineers in Bangkok, product-led culture with deep experimentation roots

---

| Area | What's Happening |

---

## PM Culture Signals

- **Experimentation-first**: Agoda runs hundreds of A/B tests simultaneously; PMs are expected to own experiment design, metric selection, and significance thresholds
- **Data-driven decisions**: No gut-feel shipping — everything is tied to conversion, booking rate, repeat rate, LTV metrics
- **Fast iteration**: Typical PM is expected to ship weekly, not quarterly
- **Ownership mindset**: PMs own outcomes, not just roadmaps; expected to partner directly with data science and engineering
- **Cross-functional authority**: PMs lead without authority across design, data, eng, and commercial teams

---

## What Agoda SPM JDs Typically Emphasize

1. **Consumer product experience** — funnel optimization, conversion rate, search-to-book flow
2. **A/B testing proficiency** — designing experiments, statistical literacy, interpreting results
3. **Data fluency** — SQL or equivalent, ability to pull own analysis without always waiting for data science
4. **Marketplace thinking** — supply + demand dynamics, pricing, inventory
5. **Cross-functional leadership** — managing without authority, aligning commercial, tech, and design
6. **Growth mindset** — acquisition, activation, retention, referral mechanics
7. **Global product sense** — multi-currency, multi-language, SEA + global user behavior

## Red Flags / Considerations

- Compensation: Bangkok salaries are lower in absolute terms but purchasing power is favorable; negotiate in USD or THB with relocation package
