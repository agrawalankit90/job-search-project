## Decision Framework (If Offer Arrives)

Accept if:
- Total comp ≥ [YOUR_COMP_FLOOR] (or THB equivalent with relocation)
- Role is Senior PM or above with real ownership (not support/shadow PM)
- Relocation support confirmed (flights, housing stipend, visa sponsorship)

Negotiate harder if:
- Base is below floor but role/scope is right — push for signing bonus or housing allowance to bridge

Decline if:
- Role scope is diminished (e.g., "PM" not "Senior PM"), comp is non-negotiable below floor, OR a materially better offer is in hand from Tier 1 target
